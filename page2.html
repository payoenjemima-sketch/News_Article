Digital Burnout: The Silent Epidemic
PHOTO — Constant screen time is affecting the mental well-being of students.

The exhaustion of the student mind has become a major concern. While the internet offers limitless knowledge, it also demands limitless attention.

“I feel like I’m always online,” one scholar shared. The boundaries between school and home life have blurred significantly.

Experts now warn of rising stress. Finding a "digital detox" is no longer just a luxury—it is becoming a vital necessity for survival.
