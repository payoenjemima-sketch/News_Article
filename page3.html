The Rise of Student Freelancers
PORTRAIT — A young developer balances high-stakes deadlines with midterms.

Economic shifts have birthed a new class of worker: the student freelancer. Trading leisure for global contracts, these students are redefining education.

“I can earn while studying,” says one freelancer. With skills in high demand, many secure their futures before they graduate.

Balancing professional deadlines with academic excellence remains a challenging but ultimately rewarding journey.
